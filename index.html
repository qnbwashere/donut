WebGL 2.0 is blocked

Your browser has GPU acceleration disabled, which Minecraft needs.
This is easy to fix — follow the steps below.

1
Open a new tab and go to
chrome://flags/#ignore-gpu-blocklist
2
Set the flag to Enabled and click Relaunch
3
Come back to this page — the game will load

If chrome://flags is blocked by your school, try on a personal device or phone.
